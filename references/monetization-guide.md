# Monetisation Guide

## Revenue streams
Advertising/YPP, sponsorships, affiliate, services, consulting, digital products, courses/workshops, memberships, events, lead generation and owned products.

## Fit assessment
Rank by audience need, trust, purchase intent, creator capability, effort, margin, risk and time to first revenue. Distinguish platform eligibility from business strategy.

## Sponsor research
Verify sponsor relationships from actual videos/descriptions and dated evidence. Do not fabricate rates. Build a rate card from creator-provided analytics, comparable verified deals or transparent assumptions.

## Funnel
Content → useful free resource → owned audience/lead → relevant offer → follow-up. Do not recommend unrelated products or manipulative monetisation.
