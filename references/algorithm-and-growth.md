# YouTube Growth and Recommendation Systems

## Practical model
Treat YouTube discovery as multiple surfaces with different viewer intent: Home/Browse, Suggested, Search, Shorts feed, subscriptions and external. Do not describe a single simplistic algorithm.

## Creator diagnostics
- Packaging: title/thumbnail promise and audience match.
- Satisfaction: whether viewers receive the promised value; use actual analytics when available.
- Session/context: format, topic, viewer history and competing content may affect distribution.
- Search: relevance, query intent, freshness and demonstrated viewer satisfaction matter; tags are not a magic ranking lever.

## Testing cascade
1. Confirm topic/audience fit and a compelling promise.
2. Test packaging hypotheses.
3. Inspect opening retention and promise confirmation.
4. Inspect mid-video retention for confusing, repetitive or low-value beats.
5. Inspect satisfaction proxies and returning viewers only when data exists.

Do not present universal CTR or retention benchmarks as guarantees. Compare against the channel's own historical baseline, format, traffic source and audience. Recommendation-system details change; verify current official guidance before making time-sensitive claims.
