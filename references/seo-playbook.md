# Video SEO Playbook

## Intent
Determine whether viewers seek a tutorial, comparison, troubleshooting answer, review, explanation or entertainment. Match the format to intent.

## Titles
Specificity and viewer payoff first. Use natural language, avoid keyword stuffing, and ensure the title accurately describes the video. Generate alternatives with different angles rather than superficial synonyms.

## Description
The opening lines explain the viewer benefit. Add useful context, sources, chapters, links and disclosures. Do not keyword-stuff.

## Tags
Use only relevant tags where they help disambiguation. Do not claim tags have a guaranteed ranking impact.

## Chapters
Use timestamps from the final edit/transcript. Do not fabricate timestamps before runtime is known.

## Metadata QA
Check title-thumbnail synergy, promise accuracy, links, disclosures, chapters, playlist, audience settings and end-screen destination. Validate current platform specifications when needed.
