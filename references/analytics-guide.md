# Analytics Diagnostic Guide

## Funnel
Impressions → CTR → views → opening retention → average view duration/watch time → satisfaction/returning viewers → conversion.

## Interpret cautiously
- Low impressions: topic, audience fit, channel history or distribution context; do not assume SEO alone.
- Impressions but weak CTR: investigate audience, title, thumbnail, topic promise and traffic source.
- Strong CTR but early drop: packaging may overpromise, opening may be slow or audience mismatch.
- Strong opening but mid-video decline: inspect pacing, clarity, repetition and payoff timing.
- Views but weak conversion: CTA, offer, audience intent or destination mismatch.

Use channel and format baselines. Public view counts cannot reveal CTR, retention or revenue. State data coverage and sample size. Prioritise one experiment at a time.
