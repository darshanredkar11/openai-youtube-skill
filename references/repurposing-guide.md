# Repurposing Guide

Repurpose ideas and value, not just file formats. A long video may support short clips, a newsletter, blog, LinkedIn post, X thread, community post or podcast outline when each platform's context is respected.

Every asset must preserve meaning, include sufficient context, have its own hook and CTA, and be checked for copyright/licensing. Short clips should be self-contained. Do not auto-publish; require review and approval.
