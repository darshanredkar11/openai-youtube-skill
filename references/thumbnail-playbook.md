# Thumbnail and Packaging Playbook

A thumbnail communicates one visual idea at small size. Build it around one focal point, clear contrast, readable expression/object/action and simple composition.

## Concept variables
- Focal subject and action
- Emotional or informational contrast
- Background and visual hierarchy
- Crop and mobile legibility
- Optional short text (usually 0–4 words)
- Title relationship: complement, don't duplicate

Generate genuinely different concepts and state the hypothesis for each. Don't claim CTR improvement without an experiment. Avoid misleading visuals and copied competitor designs.
